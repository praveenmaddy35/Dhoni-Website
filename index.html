From Ranchi to the world

The boy who became Thala

Born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, Dhoni grew up playing football as a goalkeeper before a school coach put him behind the stumps. He worked as a ticket collector at Kharagpur railway station while chasing the dream on weekend pitches.

He debuted for India in December 2004 with long hair and a heavy bat. A few months later, a blistering 148 against Pakistan in Visakhapatnam announced him — and a 183* against Sri Lanka in Jaipur confirmed he was here to stay.

What followed was a captaincy built on calm: a World T20 win in 2007, India's first No.1 Test ranking in 2009, the 2011 World Cup sealed with a six, and the 2013 Champions Trophy. In yellow, he turned Chennai Super Kings into a family and won five IPL titles.

He left international cricket quietly on 15 August 2020, and in November 2026 hung up his IPL pads too — staying on with CSK as mentor.
